Our Implementation Process

  1. 01

    Discovery & Requirements Mapping

    We visit your facilities (or conduct virtual workshops) to understand your workflows. We document your current processes, pain points, and integration needs. This phase typically takes [PLACEHOLDER: 3-5 days] for Business Systems or [PLACEHOLDER: 1-2 weeks] for schools with multiple campuses.

  2. 02

    Configuration & Customization

    We adapt our standard platform to your needs: custom fields, approval workflows, chart of accounts structures (for ERP), or curriculum mappings. Minimal customization means faster deployment and lower upgrade costs down the line.

  3. 03

    Data Migration & Integration

    We migrate your existing data with validation reports, reconcile discrepancies before go-live, and integrate with third-party systems: banking APIs, M-Pesa Daraja, email servers (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace), and legacy hardware (barcode scanners, biometric attendance machines).

  4. 04

    User Training & Go-Live Support

    We conduct hands-on training sessions for all user groups: administrators, staff members, cashiers, and students. Role-specific quick reference guides accompany the sessions. We provide hypercare support during the first [PLACEHOLDER: 4-8] weeks after go-live.

  5. 05

    Post-Implementation Optimization

    Three months after launch, we review system utilization and recommend enhancements. We help you configure new modules or departments as your business grows.
